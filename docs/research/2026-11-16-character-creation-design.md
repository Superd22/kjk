# Character creation design in modern games

This note collects what the best-regarded character creators do, seen from the player's side. It was written on 2026-11-16 to help decide whether kjk should replace its current creator. Today kjk's creator lets the player pick a premade portrait, a gender and a name. One portrait selects one appearance row, which fixes the whole body and the head together.

Every claim links to its source. Most links go to the studio, the publisher or the platform store. Where only a press article or a fan guide was available, the text says so with the words "secondary source".

## How much players care

Larian published numbers for the opening weekend of Baldur's Gate 3 in August 2023. 93% of players started their first game with a custom character instead of one of the premade Origin characters. Players spent a combined 88 years in the character creator over that weekend. 10% of the characters made in the creator took more than one hour to make. ([Larian, Community Update 23](https://baldursgate3.game/news/community-update-23-here-s-to-you_83))

By December 2023 the cumulative time spent in the Baldur's Gate 3 creator had reached 8,196 years, or about 71.6 million hours (secondary source, [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/baldurs-gate-3-players-have-clocked-over-8196-years-in-the-character-creator)). At the one-year mark, over 93% of players still chose to make their own character (secondary source, [Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/news/2024/08/baldurs-gate-3-reveals-utterly-ridiculous-player-stats-for-one-year-anniversary)).

Not every player wants to spend that time. In 2011 BioWare said that 13% of Mass Effect players used the default male Commander Shepard without changes. It also said that 83% customized the hero in some way, and 18% played a female Shepard (secondary source, [Engadget citing VG247](https://www.engadget.com/2011/07/19/bioware-13-of-mass-effect-players-use-default-hero-18-play-f/)). A good creator therefore has to serve two groups. One group wants to start playing within a minute. The other group will spend an hour or more.

Player choices also tend to cluster on a few options. During Baldur's Gate 3 early access, Larian built a character from the most-picked option in every category. It told players "Congratulations, you've basically made the default Vault Dweller" (secondary source, [Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/games/baldurs-gate-3-character-creator-test-results), which links Larian's Steam post).

## Range of control

### Presets

Starfield's creator opens with 40 premade profiles in its Biometric ID tab. The player can keep one as it is or use it as a starting point for further changes. ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/08/31/starfield-starters-customizing-perfect-captain/))

Mass Effect Legendary Edition uses one creator for all three games. The female Shepard face from Mass Effect 3 became the default female look in all three games. ([EA press release](https://news.ea.com/press-releases/press-releases-details/2021/Relive-the-Award-Winning-Space-Opera-in-Mass-Effect-Legendary-Edition-on-May-14/default.aspx))

Presets matter because they are the fast path. A player who picks a preset and presses confirm has made a choice in a few seconds. A player who wants more control uses the same preset as a starting point.

### Sliders and blend controls

Dragon Age: The Veilguard has sliders for the forehead, brow, cheeks, jaw, chin, larynx and scalp. It has further sliders for the eyes, nose, mouth and ears. The body has sliders for height, shoulder width, chest, glutes, bulge and hip width. ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator), press article with developer interviews)

The Veilguard also has a control called the Body Morpher. The player moves a point inside a triangle, and the body blends between three preset bodies at the triangle's corners. ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator))

Starfield uses a similar idea with a circle. The player places a point between muscular, thin and heavy builds. ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/08/31/starfield-starters-customizing-perfect-captain/))

A blend control like this is easier to use than five separate body sliders. One drag gives a believable whole body, and it is hard to produce a broken shape.

### Direct sculpting

Fallout 4 let the player drag parts of the face directly with the mouse or analog stick instead of moving sliders (secondary source, [MCV/UK](https://mcvuk.com/development-news/e3-2015-fallout-4-running-on-next-gen-version-of-creation-engine/) and [PC World](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2935813/bethesdas-e3-2015-kick-off-fallout-4-release-date-doom-dishonored-2-and-more.html)). The player builds the character while looking in a mirror in the opening scene (secondary source, [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2015/06/14/everything-bethesda-announced-about-fallout-at-e3-2015.aspx)). Black Desert Online is widely described in the press as the deepest creator of its time, and it also uses direct dragging on the face and body (secondary source, [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/3442-black-desert-has-an-impressive-character-creation-system)).

Direct sculpting feels natural with a mouse. It is harder with a controller, and it gives fewer guard rails than sliders with fixed ranges.

### Option counts

The inZOI Character Studio offered more than 250 options. They included hair length and style, shirt sleeves, age, fingernails, height, body type and complexion. ([KRAFTON press release](https://press.krafton.com/KRAFTON-Launches-inZOI-Character-Studio-on-Steam))

## Body type, gender, pronouns and voice

The modern pattern is to treat body, voice and pronouns as separate choices.

- The Sims 4 removed gender limits in Create a Sim in June 2016. A Sim of any gender can now have any walk, voice and body build. More than 700 sets of clothing, accessories and hairstyles became available to every gender. ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2016/06/04/the-sims-4-loosens-gender-restrictions-for-customization-options.aspx), quoting EA) EA said "The Sims is made by a diverse team for a diverse audience" in the same announcement.
- The Sims 4 added custom pronouns on 24 May 2022. The player can choose they/them, she/her, he/him or a custom set. A custom set has five grammatical forms. EA said the update does not make the game fully gender neutral, and words like "mother" and "father" stay gendered. ([EA](https://www.ea.com/en/games/the-sims/the-sims-4/news/customizable-pronouns-now-available-in-the-sims-4))
- Dragon Age: The Veilguard lets the player choose she/her, he/him or they/them separately from the body. It offers four voices. They are an English or American accent, each in a masculine or a feminine version, and each voice has a pitch control. ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator))
- Starfield lets the player set pronouns in the final step, next to the name. ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/08/31/starfield-starters-customizing-perfect-captain/))
- Cyberpunk 2077 asks for a body type instead of a gender, and it asks for a voice tone separately. The voice tone decides which pronouns other characters use for V. The combination of body type and voice also decides which romances are available. (Secondary source, [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/cyberpunk-2077-gender-options) and the [Cyberpunk fan wiki](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberpunk_2077_Character_Customization))
- Elden Ring uses the neutral labels "Body Type A" and "Body Type B" (secondary source, [Looper](https://www.looper.com/794056/the-two-ways-to-change-your-appearance-in-elden-ring/)).

For a game with voiced or written dialogue, the pronoun choice has a real cost. Every line that refers to the player by pronoun needs a version for each choice. EA's own note on The Sims shows that some gendered words can remain even after this work.

## Skin, hair, tattoos and makeup

Dragon Age: The Veilguard has the most detailed public list.

- It has 40 skin complexions. They include smooth, rugged, youthful and freckled skins. Each can be set to a cool, neutral or warm hue, with an undertone and a melanin slider.
- It has a vitiligo slider for intensity and coverage.
- It has more than 30 makeup options, including eyeliner, eyeshadow, lip colour, blush and glitter.
- Tattoos, scars and paint can be placed on the face, body, arms and legs, each with an intensity setting. Some tattoos belong to a lineage, such as designs that only elves have.
- Hair uses the Frostbite Strand system, which simulates individual strands. Qunari horns have more than 40 shapes and materials.

All of these come from [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator). Game director Corinne Busche told Edge that the series had not handled skin tones well before, and that the team wanted "to do a make-good here" (secondary source, [GamesRadar quoting Edge](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/dragon-age/dragon-age-the-veilguard-director-wants-the-rpgs-character-creator-to-be-a-make-good-full-disclosure-dragon-age-has-traditionally-not-done-skin-tones-well)).

Mass Effect Legendary Edition also expanded its selection of hair, makeup and skin tones over the original trilogy ([EA press release](https://news.ea.com/press-releases/press-releases-details/2021/Relive-the-Award-Winning-Space-Opera-in-Mass-Effect-Legendary-Edition-on-May-14/default.aspx)).

inZOI goes further on clothing. Its AI Texture tool uses generative AI to create new patterns for a character's clothes. ([KRAFTON press release](https://press.krafton.com/KRAFTON-Launches-inZOI-Character-Studio-on-Steam))

Fighting games put their depth in outfits and decals instead of faces. Soulcalibur VI sold several Character Creation sets as downloadable content. Set C, for example, adds 43 items, including 14 hit effects and 10 stickers. Set D adds 48 items, including 14 hit effects and 11 stickers. ([Bandai Namco](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/node/5092))

## Preview conditions

A creator is only as good as what the player can see while using it. Dragon Age: Inquisition's creator was criticized for dim, green-tinted lighting. The Veilguard answers this with four lighting scenes that the player can switch between before confirming the look. Creative director John Epler said the team knew many players were unhappy with Inquisition's lighting. ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator))

Monster Hunter Wilds shows the other side of this. Its makeup and face paint have a glow setting. During the open beta, players used it to make faces that looked like light sources, for example to recreate characters from other games (secondary source, [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/monster-hunter-wilds-character-creator-is-being-used-to-make-scarily-accurate-recreations-of-superheroes-actors-and-characters-from-other-games/)). Players will push every control to its limits, and the creator has to look right in the game's real lighting, not only in the studio light.

## Sharing and importing

- Mass Effect Legendary Edition accepts face codes from Mass Effect 2 and Mass Effect 3. EA warned that some codes do not match exactly and may need manual changes ([EA forums, Gameplay Calibrations](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/mass-effect-franchise-discussion-en/gameplay-calibrations/9083822)).
- Saints Row (2022) gives each saved Boss a 7-character share code. Other players type the code to download the Boss (secondary source, [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Saints-Row/archives/385223)).
- The Sims 4 Gallery lets players upload and download households, rooms and lots. EA warns that a download loses any custom or pack content the downloading player does not own ([EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/help/the-sims/the-sims-4/the-sims-4-gallery/)).
- inZOI's Canvas platform lets players upload creations, download other players' creations, like them and comment on them. The ten most popular creations appear at the top of a ranking ([KRAFTON](https://press.krafton.com/KRAFTON-Launches-inZOI-Character-Studio-on-Steam)).
- Black Desert Online's Beauty Album lets players upload a look to a gallery that every player can browse. Other players can endorse a look and apply it to their own character (secondary source, [Black Desert Mobile wiki](https://www.world.blackdesertm.com/Ocean/Wiki?wikiNo=34) and [Gamingcypher](https://gamingcypher.com/black-desert-online-character-designs-can-be-shared-swapped-with-the-beauty-album)).
- WWE 2K26 doubled its Create-a-Superstar slots to 200 and its Create-an-Image slots to 2,000. The same report says Switch 2 players can use the Image Uploader and the Joy-Con 2 mouse controls to paint the body ([2K newsroom](https://newsroom.2k.com/news/players-creativity-takes-center-stage-as-wwer-2k26-universe-and-community-creations-details-revealed-in-ringside-report-narrated-by-bayley)).

A short text code is the cheapest sharing method. It needs no server, it works in a chat message, and it can be printed in a forum post.

## How the creator ties into roleplay

The best RPG creators make the player decide who the character is, not only what they look like.

- Starfield has 21 backgrounds. 20 are named, and one is a hidden option labelled "[File Not Found]". Each background gives three starting skills. The player can also take up to three of 17 traits, or none. Each trait has an upside and a downside, and some traits exclude each other ([Bethesda](https://bethesda.net/en-US/news/build-your-ideal-starship-captain-in-starfield), [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/08/31/starfield-starters-customizing-perfect-captain/)). The Dream Home trait, for example, gives the player a house with a 125,000 credit mortgage that must be paid weekly ([Bethesda Support](https://help.bethesda.net/app/answers/detail/a_id/61028/~/character-traits---starfield)).
- Dragon Age: The Veilguard asks for a lineage, a class and one of six factions. The faction acts as the character's background. Game director Corinne Busche said "Rook is here because they choose to be." ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator))
- Dragon's Dogma 2 ties the body to gameplay. Height and weight change carrying capacity and stamina recovery. A heavier character carries more and recovers stamina more slowly (secondary source, [Automaton](https://automaton-media.com/en/news/in-dragons-dogma-2-your-characters-physique-will-affect-the-game/)). The same creator also sets the Main Pawn's inclination, which decides how the companion behaves in combat ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2674810)).
- Baldur's Gate 3 offers a custom character, several premade Origin characters, and the Dark Urge. The Dark Urge is a custom-looking character with a fixed and dark backstory. 15% of players chose the Dark Urge in the first year (secondary source, [Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/news/2024/08/baldurs-gate-3-reveals-utterly-ridiculous-player-stats-for-one-year-anniversary)).
- Street Fighter 6 uses the creator to make an avatar for its World Tour story mode. The same avatar then appears in the Battle Hub online lobby (secondary source, [Upcomer](https://upcomer.com/does-street-fighter-6-have-character-creation-explained)).

Games tend to lock the roleplay choices and leave the looks open. The Veilguard locks class, lineage and identity but allows any appearance change ([Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive/2024/06/13/a-deep-dive-into-dragon-age-the-veilguards-expansive-character-creator)). Baldur's Gate 3 locks race and body type but allows the rest (secondary source, [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/137161/baldurs-gate-3-patch-3-notes)).

## Speed and accessibility

The common tools for players who want to go fast are these.

- A set of presets, as in Starfield's 40 profiles ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/08/31/starfield-starters-customizing-perfect-captain/)).
- A premade character with a story, as with Baldur's Gate 3's Origins, chosen by 7% of players at launch ([Larian](https://baldursgate3.game/news/community-update-23-here-s-to-you_83)).
- A good default. BioWare's figure that 13% of Mass Effect players kept the default male Shepard shows that a default will be used, so it has to look good (secondary source, [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2011/07/19/bioware-13-of-mass-effect-players-use-default-hero-18-play-f/)).
- A free mirror later in the game. The player can confirm a rough look now and fix it after a few hours of play, as in Baldur's Gate 3, Cyberpunk 2077, Elden Ring and the Veilguard (sources in the table above).
- A creator released before launch, so that the long session happens before the game starts (sources in the launch table above).

## What this means for a KOTOR-style RPG

KOTOR's choices already include a class, a gender, a portrait and a name. The portrait picks a full appearance. The takeaways below are listed from cheapest to most expensive.

1. Keep the portraits as presets. Every studio above still offers a fast path. A player who wants the old KOTOR flow should be able to pick a portrait and press confirm, exactly as today.
2. Split the head from the body. KOTOR's appearance table already names the head model separately from the body model. Letting the player pick a head and a body separately multiplies the number of looks at very little cost. A preset then fills both, and the player can change either one.
3. Add skin tone, hair and hair colour, and a few tattoo and scar overlays. The Veilguard shows that skin tone range matters to players and that its absence is noticed. Overlays are textures, so they do not need new head models.
4. Keep body type, voice and pronouns as separate choices only where the game can support them. KOTOR's dialogue was written and voiced for a male or female player character. Cyberpunk's approach of tying pronouns to one voice choice may fit KOTOR better than a free pronoun choice. Any free pronoun choice would require changing every line that refers to the player.
5. Preview the character under several lighting scenes, holding a drawn lightsaber, and in the class's starting armour. The Veilguard's four lighting scenes answered a known complaint. In a game where the player will mostly see the character in robes or armour, a bare-skin preview misleads.
6. Add a free mirror. A mirror or a medical droid on the Ebon Hawk could reopen the appearance part of the creator at no cost. Class, attributes and the player's identity should stay locked, as in the Veilguard and Baldur's Gate 3. The Monster Hunter Wilds reversal shows that charging for this is unpopular.
7. Add a share code. A short text code that encodes the head, body, colours and overlays is cheap to build. It lets players post their characters without any server.
8. Consider a background choice that has a small mechanical effect. Starfield's backgrounds each give three starting skills, and its traits each have an upside and a downside. A KOTOR background could set one or two starting skills or a starting feat, and the dialogue could reference it in a few places. This fits KOTOR's existing skill and feat system without new systems.
9. Make the default look good. At least 13% of Mass Effect players never changed the default Shepard. The first portrait shown for each gender and class will be the face of the game for many players.
10. Leave sculpting and per-vertex morphs for last. They are the most expensive item on this list, they need new head rigs and new animation work, and the evidence above suggests that presets, colours, overlays and a free mirror deliver most of what players value.
