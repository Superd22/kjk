Eye parts

Lid pieces are the separate upper eyelids that sit over the eyeball, on KOTOR's head and on ours. On our head the lash strip goes with them, and the skin the piece covers is shown again. The caruncle is the pink piece in our eye's inner corner. With the eye paint off, both heads' eyeballs are one plain light colour. "Shape only" hides all three and also takes the paint off the skin, because painted lashes and shadows draw a dark band round the eye. Only the opening's outline and the skin and eyeball around it are left to compare. "Show all" puts the earlier skin back.
