kjk head customization · status on 19 November 2026

Likeness HQ

The goal. Players get a head customization system that is varied enough to be interesting, and that fits KOTOR's look and feel.

The bet. We think the FLAME head model, with controls of our own added to it, can be the base of that system. This page tracks how we prove it. The human heads of KOTOR 1 and 2 are the test set, because they show the variety KOTOR's look allows. There are 57 test heads: 45 heads from the games, Kreia, Bastila and 12 heads from mods.

The plan. Part A holds what must be true for the whole head. Part B takes the face zone by zone. Everything else is tooling, listed at the end. The full roadmap is docs/research/2026-11-19-head-roadmap.md in the kjk repo.

BZone by zone

Each zone of the face goes through five steps. The zones will probably be split further once the work reaches them.

  • B1. Variety. Does KOTOR vary enough in this zone? We measure how far apart two KOTOR heads are in the zone, on average.
  • B2. Reproduce. Do our fitted heads keep that variety? They must differ from each other as much as the KOTOR heads do, and the same heads must differ most. Each fit must also land close to its own KOTOR head.
  • B3. Fix. Add controls or learned shapes for what we cannot reproduce.
  • B4. Paint. Today we carry each KOTOR head's own texture onto our head. A customizer needs its own painting system.
  • B5. Shippable. The zone still works with fewer vertices, and through sliders a player can use. When this happens is still open.

KOTOR's zones do vary. Two KOTOR mouths are 2.1 mm apart on average, and two foreheads 3.6 mm. Our fits keep between 93% and 102% of that variety in every zone. The heads that differ most in KOTOR also differ most in ours, with a correlation between 0.91 and 0.99. In every zone, the fit error is between 22% and 34% of the variety. Around the eyes is the zone that keeps the least, at 93%.

All 57 fitted heads lie nearer to their own KOTOR head than to any other, over the whole face. On a typical head the nearest other KOTOR head is 0.65 mm farther than its own.

Lined up on the whole face, a zone's variety mixes its own shape with where it sits on the head. The grid also gives each zone lined up on itself, which leaves only its shape. The mouth changes the most. Two KOTOR mouths are 2.08 mm apart on the whole face, but their shapes alone only 1.00 mm. Our fits keep 88% of that shape variety, the least of any zone. The nose keeps 90%, and every other zone 92% or more.

Whether a zone varies "enough" is a judgement by eye, and finally in the engine, not a number. The galleries are made for that judgement. Each shows all 57 heads cropped to one zone, numbered the same way on every sheet. So far there are galleries for the mouth only.

Around the eyes KOTOR 1.92 mm apart · fit 0.64 mm off · 51 of 57 within 0.75 mm

This is the only zone with new controls so far. Each eye has 26 controls for its opening, and our lid edge lies 0.87 mm from KOTOR's eye slit on average, against 5.85 mm with FLAME alone. The blink is computed on each head, and all 57 close. The lid paint and the lash strip are paused. The details are in docs/research/2026-11-19-eyelids-and-lashes.md.

This zone keeps the least variety, 93%. Some opening controls hit their limits on up to 36 heads, which may be why.

Mouth KOTOR 2.08 mm apart, 1.00 mm in shape alone · fit 0.46 mm off · 56 of 57 within 0.75 mm

Lined up on itself, so that only its shape counts, two KOTOR mouths differ by 1.0 mm on average. Our fits keep 88% of that, and the heads whose mouths differ most in KOTOR also differ most in ours (correlation 0.95). Our fit error, 0.46 mm, is close to half of KOTOR's mouth variety. So the mouth is the zone where our error matters most against KOTOR's variety.

In the pictures, KOTOR's lips have a crisp, sharp border. Ours are softer, and look more alike from head to head. Our lip line is jagged where the lips meet, which is a fault of our mesh. The distance maps are speckled red and green a few millimetres apart. That probably follows KOTOR's flat triangles, which a smooth surface cannot lie on. This guess is not checked yet.

Worst heads: pmhc04, p_joleeh and n_zezkaiellh.
