Nose, jaw and chin sliders

This page tests the features built the new way. The nose, and the jaw and chin, each have sliders. A feature is measured by its proportions and outlines on KOTOR's own head meshes. The sliders try to make every KOTOR nose, jaw and chin, and the result is shown next to the original.

The pass marks are provisional. They were set from how much KOTOR's noses, jaws and chins vary and how much the measure wobbles, not from what you can see. The tab "Same or different nose" asks you one question at a time about the nose. Under "Made against original", every card has a control to mark the nose, or the jaw and chin, as the same as KOTOR's or not the same. Until you have answered or marked, every pass and fail on this page uses the provisional marks.

All 57 test heads, on KOTOR's own meshes with their own paint, sorted by one measure of the nose, or of the jaw and chin. Each row should run in order. If an order looks wrong to you, the measure is wrong there.

Proving the measure

Two noses are shown side by side, on the same head and from the same camera. Say whether they look like the same nose or two different noses. There is no right answer, and your first impression is what counts.

Loading the first question.
You can turn both noses to another view with these buttons or the keys 1 to 4. Turning them does not count as an answer. The key S answers "Same nose" and the key D answers "Different nose".

Pass marks

This was the first way to set the pass marks, with round 1's measure. It is kept with its answers as a record. The tab "Same or different nose" replaces it.

The base head's nose changes in 10 even steps along one measure, while the other proportions stay where they are. Step 0 is the base head. Click the first step where you see that the nose differs from step 0. That step becomes the pass mark for that measure.
