This checks roadmap item A1 for the mouth. The test plays KOTOR's own face clips on our fitted heads, with KOTOR's face bones. The skin weights come from our rig and follow each vertex. The bones are placed on each head's own lips, mouth corners, cheeks, brows and eyes. Each head shows eight poses: at rest, five lip sync shapes from the "talk" clip, a laugh and a sad face.

  • On all ten heads the mouth opens, rounds, closes and smiles the way it does on our base head. So the rig carries over to a fitted head.
  • The open shapes part the lips by 7.6 mm to 10.5 mm at the middle of the mouth, against 8.2 mm to 8.8 mm on the base head.
  • The rounded shapes ("OW OY" and "UH UW W") turn over up to 12 small triangles at the mouth corners. The base head turns over 8. The pressed shape ("B M P") turns over 16 to 18 on every head, base included, where the lips press together and roll inward.
  • The gap number reads below 0 for "B M P" on every head. In the pictures the lips are pressed together, as they should be, so the number is wrong for this shape and needs a better measure.
  • The inside of the mouth stays behind the lips on nine heads. On Jolee it comes 0.7 mm in front of them in "UH UW W".

The paint inside the mouth is wrong. Our head has no teeth yet, and the paint transfer smears KOTOR's teeth, gums and beard onto the inside of our mouth. That is a painting task (part C).
