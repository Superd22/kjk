Compare a head

Pick any of the 57 heads. The left view is the KOTOR head as the game draws it. The right view is our fitted head. The Paint menu chooses what it wears: KOTOR's paint, or one of our own procedural paints. The switch "Our shape on both sides" turns the left view into our head with KOTOR's paint, so the two views differ only in paint. Drag either view to turn both heads together, and scroll to zoom. Both heads play the chosen animation at the same time. Each head moves with its own skin weights. The left head uses the weights that its KOTOR model file stores.
