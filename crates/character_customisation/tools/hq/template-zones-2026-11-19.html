kjk head customization · status on 19 November 2026

Likeness HQ

This page shows how far our head system is from reproducing the human heads of KOTOR 1 and 2, and every step that is still ahead. It is updated after each session of face work.

The goal. Our head is the FLAME head model, plus controls we add to it. It must reproduce every human head in KOTOR 1 and 2, in KOTOR's style. Then players must be able to edit it through sliders that are easy to use.

Only 3D heads are used as tests now, not photos. The test set is 57 heads: 45 heads from the games, plus Kreia and Bastila, and 12 heads from mods. Random slider settings are not used as tests. The values that the 57 fits use will set each slider's range later.

The pass mark. A head passes when three things are true. The whole face lies within 0.5 mm of the KOTOR head on average. No zone of the face is worse than 0.75 mm. And our head is closer to its own KOTOR head than to any other. The mark may be tightened later if the heads still do not look right.

The final judge. A head is only done when you judge it feature by feature in the kjk engine, with our head wearing the character's own KOTOR texture. The pictures on this page come from our test renderer, so they only check the method.

On average only the forehead is far from its mark. But no single head passes. None has its whole face within 0.5 mm, and only 2 of the 57 have every zone within 0.75 mm. KOTOR models the hair as part of the skull, so the forehead's score includes hair. Step 4 deals with that. The Zones section below goes through each zone in turn.

Zones

This section has one part for each zone of the face. Each part gives a verdict on one question. Do our sliders reproduce this zone on the KOTOR heads? A zone is reproduced when its mean distance is within the mark of 0.75 mm, and when most of the 57 heads are within it too. A zone is close when its mean is within 0.15 mm of the mark. Otherwise it is not reproduced.

The numbers are the mean and the 95th percentile over the 57 heads, as in the chart above. The first value is for FLAME with the eye opening controls, which is the current state. The second value is for FLAME alone. A proportion is how much longer a part is on our head than on KOTOR's, in millimetres. A negative value means ours is shorter.

What is done

  • Each eye has 26 new controls for the opening's width, its place sideways, and the height and depth of the lid edge at seven stops along the lids.
  • Our lid edge now lies 0.87 mm from KOTOR's slit on average, against 5.85 mm with FLAME alone. The opening's height is 0.07 mm off.
  • The blink is computed on each head from its own lid edges. All 57 heads close with less than 1 mm left between the lids.

What we still dislike

  • A closing lid shows the liner and lash paint taken from the open lid, so it looks smeared and dark. This is worst on Bastila, pfha02 and Meetra.
  • The lash strip does not exist yet. Bastila's and Kreia's lower liner shows doubled, and Bastila's liner wing cannot be made.
  • The inner corner's depth controls hit their limit on 31 to 36 of the 57 heads. The sideways controls hit their limit on 10 to 13 heads.
  • The opening is still about 1 mm narrow on average, because both corners stop about 1 mm short of KOTOR's.
  • On mod_hp_pfha01 our lid edge lies 7.18 mm from KOTOR's slit. The next worst head is 2.1 mm off, so this target may be faulty.
  • A small dent shows above the outer corner when the eye closes.

Next

  • Paint the lid skin with plain skin from above the eye.
  • Build the lash strip, a thin strip on the lid edge that carries the lashes and the liner, with its own sliders outside FLAME.
  • Widen the ranges of the depth and sideways controls, and check the target of mod_hp_pfha01.
  • Fix the dent above the outer corner.

1The KOTOR test heads

Done

What is done

  • The games hold 194 face meshes, which come down to 48 distinct shapes. We use 45 game heads, plus Kreia and Bastila, plus 12 mod heads.
  • Our head is wrapped onto each KOTOR head. Hair, the mesh's open edges and the inside of the mouth are left out.
  • Our lid edge goes onto KOTOR's eye slit. On all 57 heads it lands within 0.76 mm of its place.

What we still dislike

  • On the mod head mod_hp_pfha01 our lid edge ends up 7.2 mm from KOTOR's slit. The next worst head is 2.1 mm off, so this head's target may be faulty.
  • Kreia, mod_hp_pfha01 and mod_hp_pfhb02 have hair panels and no skull.

Next

  • Check the target of mod_hp_pfha01, then fix it or drop it.

2A fit we can trust

Done

What is done

  • The fit is tested on heads that each slider set makes exactly. It finds FLAME's heads to 0.013 mm, our named controls' heads to 0.000 mm and MakeHuman's to 0.024 mm.
  • Since 19 November the target's size is held fixed. Before, the fit could shrink a target by up to 10% and use FLAME's size shapes instead. That left KOTOR's eye slit too small for our eyeball, which has a fixed size.

What we still dislike

  • Every fault found so far was in the targets or the method, not in the sliders. So a new measure must be checked on slider-made heads before its numbers are trusted.

Next

  • Run the slider-made head check on the new set with the eye opening controls.

5The face to the pass mark

Now

What is done

  • FLAME's 300 shapes alone bring the face to 0.70 mm. With the eye opening controls it is 0.64 mm. The average head, with no slider set, is about 2.4 mm off.
  • FLAME's lower face comes out a little bigger than KOTOR's. In the fit before the eye opening controls, its jaw was 0.8 mm wider and its face 0.7 mm longer from brow to chin. With them the jaw is 0.44 mm wider, and the face height is only 0.11 mm off.
  • 100 smooth shapes that know nothing about faces do no better than FLAME. So FLAME is not simply short of freedom.

What we still dislike

  • No head passes the mark.
  • The forehead is the worst zone at 1.08 mm, partly because of hair.
  • The brows, the chin and the jaw are each about 0.8 mm off.

Next

  • After step 4, learn new shapes from what FLAME leaves over on the KOTOR heads, which is about 0.6 mm. Test each learned shape on heads it was not learned from.
  • Check that each head lies closer to its own KOTOR head than to any other.

8From fits to sliders

Not yet

Each control's range comes from the values the 57 fits use, and the player's sliders stop at those ends. Anything that breaks beyond them is fixed or cut at that point. The plans for using fewer vertices and for naming the sliders are written in docs/research/2026-11-17-head-parts-and-controls.md. This step also decides how the game gets the closed eye: computed in the engine, or carried by the sliders.
